# THE APEX CLUB design brief

Design read: A premium Chennai fitness club for ambitious members who want expert coaching, beautiful spaces and a calm, private training experience.

Concept spine: The Apex Standard. Every section moves from arrival to assessment, training, recovery and belonging.

Delivery tier: cinema-inspired static site. The user declined video, so the experience uses restrained CSS entrance motion, image depth and interaction states instead of a film.

Animation mode: non-animated — user declined video; use optional motion without a mandatory camera journey.

Locked palette: #08090A black, #101112 charcoal, #F26B2D orange, #FF8A4C soft orange, #FFFFFF white and #B4B6B8 muted white. The APEX identity is now a sharp black, white and orange athletic system.

Locked type: Space Grotesk throughout, with bold weights for an athletic, architectural APEX identity. Body copy and interface labels share the same modern geometric sans for a more unified, confident read.

Section plan: asymmetric split hero, metric rail, editorial about, membership comparison, trainer cards, facility bento, contact form and location footer. No repeated section family.

Asset plan: generated Chennai gym interior hero, generated training room image, generated launch cover, generated favicon. Additional detail panels use the same generated gym imagery with crop variation to keep the kit coherent.

CTA inventory: "Book a private tour" is the primary consultation action. "Explore memberships" is the secondary route. Contact uses "Request a consultation".

Copy register: concise, confident, hospitality-led and specific. No invented performance claims or fake testimonials.

Responsive intent: desktop uses asymmetry and large imagery; mobile becomes a single-column reading flow with persistent access to booking and contact actions.
